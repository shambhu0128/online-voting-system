Online Voting Application

A secure, transparent, and convenient way to cast your vote anytime, anywhere. Participate in elections with confidence and ease.

✅ Secure

All votes are encrypted and stored securely to ensure transparency and accuracy.

🌐 Accessible

Vote from anywhere using your computer or mobile device. No long queues, no hassle.

📊 Transparent

Instantly view election results once voting ends. Fully automated and fair.

How It Works

  1. Register an account using your valid credentials.
  2. Login to the system and choose your preferred candidate.
  3. Cast your vote safely and securely.
  4. Admins can monitor votes and see final results after elections.
